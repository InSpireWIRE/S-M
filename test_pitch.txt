Roberto's Last Catch - Documentary Pitch

This documentary follows Roberto Martinez, a third-generation fisherman facing the end of his traditional way of life. Industrial trawlers have destroyed local fish stocks.

Act 1: We meet Roberto on his boat, struggling with mounting debts.
Act 2: The bank forecloses, Roberto loses his boat.
Act 3: Roberto accepts factory work, identity crisis deepens.

The film uses observational techniques to capture intimate moments of loss and transformation.
