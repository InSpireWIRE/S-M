# S!M THEORETICAL FOUNDATION
## Academic Research & Story Frameworks

### 1. DOCUMENTARY THEORY (Primary Framework)

#### Bill Nichols' Six Documentary Modes
*Source: "Introduction to Documentary" (2001, 2010)*

**Implemented Modes:**
1. **Expository Mode**
   - Voice of authority explaining the topic
   - Detection patterns: expert interviews, narration, evidence presentation
   - Conversational question: "So who are your experts and why should we trust them?"

2. **Observational Mode**  
   - Fly-on-the-wall, cinema vérité approach
   - Detection patterns: longitudinal following, no intervention
   - Conversational question: "How long can you realistically follow these people around?"

3. **Participatory Mode**
   - Filmmaker becomes part of the story
   - Detection patterns: filmmaker presence, on-camera involvement
   - Conversational question: "Are you ready to become part of the story?"

4. **Reflexive Mode**
   - Documentary about documentary-making
   - Detection patterns: meta-commentary, process discussion
   - Conversational question: "Why should audiences care about YOUR process?"

5. **Performative Mode**
   - Filmmaker's personal journey is the story
   - Detection patterns: personal narrative, subjective experience
   - Conversational question: "What personal truth are you exploring?"
